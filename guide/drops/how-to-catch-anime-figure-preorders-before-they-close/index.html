What are anime figure preorders?

Anime figure preorders are advance purchases of collectible figures based on popular anime characters, allowing fans to secure items before they sell out. These preorders are typically available months before the official release date, often through specialized retailers like AmiAmi, Crunchyroll, or Good Smile Company. Preorders are essential for limited-edition or high-demand figures, as they ensure availability and often come with exclusive bonuses like extra accessories or packaging. Prices for preordered figures range from $30 to $300+, depending on the size, quality, and rarity. Preorder periods usually last 1-3 months, and payment is often required upfront or upon shipment. Missing a preorder window can lead to paying inflated prices on secondary markets like eBay.

How do I find preorder announcements for anime figures?

To find preorder announcements for anime figures, follow official social media accounts of manufacturers like Good Smile Company or Kotobukiya, and subscribe to newsletters from retailers such as AmiAmi or Crunchyroll. Websites like MyFigureCollection also track upcoming releases and preorder dates. Preorders are often announced 3-6 months before release, with specific dates and times listed in Japan Standard Time (JST). For example, Good Smile Company typically opens preorders at 12 PM JST. Setting up Google Alerts for specific figure names or brands can also help. Additionally, joining anime figure communities on Reddit or Discord can provide timely updates, as members often share preorder links and announcements as soon as they go live.

Setting calendar reminders for preorder dates and times is essential, as preorders often go live at specific times, such as 12 PM JST for Good Smile Company. Browser extensions like Distill can monitor webpage changes and alert you when preorder pages update. Additionally, joining Discord servers dedicated to anime figures can provide community-driven alerts and tips, ensuring you don’t miss high-demand preorders.

How much do anime figure preorders typically cost?

Anime figure preorders typically cost between $30 and $300+, depending on the figure’s size, quality, and exclusivity. Smaller figures, like Nendoroids, usually range from $30 to $60, while larger, detailed scales can cost $150 to $300 or more. Limited-edition or exclusive figures may have higher prices due to their rarity. Shipping costs vary by retailer and location, often adding $10 to $50 depending on the figure’s size and destination. For example, AmiAmi charges approximately $20 for shipping a medium-sized figure to the US. Payment is often required upfront or upon shipment, and some retailers offer payment plans. Preordering ensures you pay the retail price, avoiding inflated costs on secondary markets.

The best websites for anime figure preorders include AmiAmi, Crunchyroll, Good Smile Company, and BigBadToyStore. When do anime figure preorders usually open?

Anime figure preorders usually open 3-6 months before the official release date, with specific times often tied to Japan Standard Time (JST). For example, Good Smile Company typically opens preorders at 12 PM JST. Major announcements often occur during events like WonFes (Wonder Festival), held twice a year in February and August. Preorder periods typically last 1-3 months, though high-demand figures may sell out within hours or days. Retailers like AmiAmi and Crunchyroll announce preorder dates on their websites and social media. Setting reminders for exact times is crucial, as preorders often go live simultaneously across multiple platforms. Missing the initial window can mean relying on secondary markets, where prices may be significantly higher.

What are common pitfalls when preordering anime figures?

Common pitfalls when preordering anime figures include missing preorder deadlines, underestimating shipping costs, and failing to verify retailer authenticity. Preorders often sell out quickly, sometimes within hours, especially for popular figures. Shipping costs can add $20-$50, depending on the figure’s size and destination. Scams are a risk, so only order from reputable retailers like AmiAmi or Good Smile Company. Cancellation policies vary; some retailers charge fees or prohibit cancellations entirely. Additionally, currency conversion fees may apply for international orders. Preordering multiple figures can lead to overspending, so set a budget. Finally, delays are common; release dates may be pushed back by months, so plan accordingly.

What are alternatives if I miss a preorder?

If you miss a preorder, alternatives include purchasing from secondary markets like eBay, Mercari, or Mandarake, though prices may be higher. Retailers like BigBadToyStore or Crunchyroll sometimes offer additional stock after preorders close. Joining waitlists or subscribing to restock notifications can help secure canceled orders. Figures may also be available at conventions like Anime Expo or through local hobby shops, though availability varies. Some manufacturers release reprints of popular figures, often announced on their social media. Prices on secondary markets can range from 10% to 100% above retail, depending on demand. For example, a $100 figure might sell for $150-$200 on eBay. Patience and persistence are key to finding missed preorders at reasonable prices.

How do I ensure I’m eligible for anime figure preorders?

To ensure eligibility for anime figure preorders, verify that the retailer ships to your country and check for age restrictions, as some figures may be labeled for ages 15+. Create accounts on websites like AmiAmi or Crunchyroll in advance, as preorders often require immediate checkout. Payment methods vary; some retailers accept credit cards, PayPal, or bank transfers. Ensure your payment information is up-to-date to avoid delays. International buyers should confirm import taxes and customs fees, which can add 10%-20% to the total cost. For example, US buyers may face a 10% customs fee on imports over $800. Read retailer policies on cancellations and refunds, as some prohibit changes after preorders are placed.
